Is quinoa good for diabetes?
Yes! Quinoa is an excellent choice for diabetes management. It has a low glycemic index (GI ~53), is high in protein (8g per 100g cooked), and provides complete essential amino acids. Its high fiber content (2.8g/100g) slows glucose absorption, helping stabilize blood sugar levels. It also contains magnesium, which is linked to improved insulin sensitivity.

Per 100g of lentil soup: ~116 kcal · Protein: 9g · Carbs: 20g · Fat: 0.4g · Fiber: 7.9g. Excellent source of folate, iron, and potassium. Cited from knowledge base: Lentil Soup entry.
